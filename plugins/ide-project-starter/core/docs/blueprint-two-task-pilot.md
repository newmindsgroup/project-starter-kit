## Stop and repair conditions

Ambiguous attribution, malformed source, stale revision, closed-time overlap, negative/regressed usage, unmatched backup totals or missing acceptance evidence leaves the affected task pending. Preserve raw evidence and inspect locally. Never turn unknowns into zeros or bypass a blocked record by blindly advancing the cursor.

## Exit gates

Two real accepted deliverables, reviewed bindings or explicitly unassigned usage, repeat-import idempotency, confirmed/corrected timing, fresh-target recovery, a clear coverage/gap report and no invented dollar savings. This pilot measures delivery evidence. Prices, market baselines, subscription allocation and local depreciation are the next economics stage.
